External Building Repair across Dubai

What's included

External repair covers the defects that let weather into a building: spalling concrete, exposed rebar, failed joints and cracked render. We repair the structure first and finish with a protective coating so the facade stays sound for years.

Al Zamin Technical Services has worked for Juma Al Majid Group, Al-Futtaim Group Real Estate, Al Naboodah, Aqaar Properties and other leading UAE property groups. Every job is run by our own engineers and technicians, and we brief you on the problem and the proposed solution before work starts.
